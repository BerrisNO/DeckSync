A page dial for decks without one. The key shows the page it goes to, with that page's name and icon from the Stream Deck app.
"Next, hold for previous" puts both directions on one key, handy on small decks.

Colours are hex codes, e.g. #FF8800, and apply to every DeckSync key and the dial strip. The accent lights the frame when the decks are in sync. Empty = default.
